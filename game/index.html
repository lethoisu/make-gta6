SOLMAR BAY

Sunset Run — an open-world crime story

Jax Marlowe owes casino king Victor Kane a fortune. Fixer Sol Reyes has five jobs that could clear the debt — if the Solmar Bay PD doesn't catch Jax first. Steal cars, lose the heat, and pull off the Big Score.

WASD / arrows — walk & drive  •  Shift — run

E — steal / leave car  •  Space — punch  •  F / click — shoot

P / Esc — pause  •  M — mute  •  R — restart

Progress saves automatically in this browser. A wholly original game — its city, characters, vehicles, story, art and music are its own.
